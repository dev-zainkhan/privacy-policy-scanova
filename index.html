Scanova Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026  |  Last updated: October 12, 2026

Scanova ("the app") is a document-scanning and PDF utility. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app, why it is handled, and the choices available to you.

Summary

  • Scanned pages, document names, and PDFs are processed and stored on your device by the app. The app does not operate a document-upload or account service.
  • If you choose to share or save a file outside the app, the file is sent or saved using the destination and method you select.
  • The app includes Google Mobile Ads. Google may collect device, advertising, interaction, and diagnostic information to provide and measure ads and help prevent fraud.
  • You can delete documents in the app. Files you have saved outside the app must be deleted from their destination separately.

Information Handled by Scanova

Documents and Files

When you scan a document, the app uses the camera through its document-scanning component. Scanned page images, page details, and document names are stored in the app's private storage on your device. The app's scanning and PDF features process these files on your device; the app does not upload your scans or imported PDFs to a Scanova server.

When you choose a PDF using the system file picker, the app receives access to the file you selected so it can perform the requested operation. The app processes the selected PDF on your device. The operating system or file provider may apply its own terms and privacy practices.

The app stores basic preferences and, on Android, may remember a folder you selected for saving PDFs. These are stored on your device.

Documents can contain personal or sensitive information that you choose to scan or import. Avoid scanning or sharing information unless you are comfortable doing so.

Google Mobile Ads

The app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for banner and interstitial advertising. Although the app currently uses Google's test ad IDs, the SDK is initialized by the app. Google's published disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK states that it may automatically collect and share:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • interactions with the app, such as app launches, taps, and ad views;
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance; and
  • device or account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, and other identifiers where applicable.

Google describes these uses as supporting advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, and states that SDK-collected data is encrypted in transit using TLS. The precise data handling may depend on the SDK version, platform, ad settings, region, and any ad products or features enabled. Google handles information it collects under its own privacy policy and terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID through your device's settings. Google's privacy policy explains additional controls for Google services and advertising.

How Information Is Shared

Scanova does not provide scanned documents or imported PDFs to an app-operated server. The app's advertising SDK sends the advertising-related information described above to Google. Google may process it as described in Google's policies.

If you use the app's Share feature, the operating system opens a sharing interface so you can choose a recipient or app. If you save a PDF to a folder, it is saved to the location you select. Those recipients, apps, and storage providers handle information under their own policies. Sharing or saving a document is your choice.

Permissions and Access

  • Camera: used when you start scanning a document. Camera access is provided by the operating system and is not used by the app for continuous background capture.
  • Selected files: the app can access PDFs you choose through the system file picker to perform the operation you requested.
  • Selected save folder (Android): if you choose to save a PDF, Android asks you to select a folder. The app uses that folder permission to create the PDF you requested and may remember the selected folder for later saves. You can revoke folder access in Android settings.

The app does not request an account, contacts, microphone, or location access in its current configuration.

Retention and Deletion

Your in-app documents and preferences remain in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app. To delete a document, use its delete control in Scanova. Removing the app normally removes its private app data, subject to your device's operating-system behavior and backups.

PDFs that you save to a user-selected folder, share with another app, or otherwise copy outside Scanova are separate copies. Delete those copies using the relevant file manager, destination app, or storage provider.

Google controls retention of information collected by its advertising services. See Google's Privacy Policy and advertising controls for details.

Security

The app stores its working documents in private app storage provided by the operating system and processes them on the device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. When Google Mobile Ads handles data, Google states that it encrypts that data in transit using TLS. Files shared or saved outside the app are subject to the security practices of the destination you choose.

Children's Privacy

Scanova is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not create user accounts or ask children to provide personal information. Google Mobile Ads may automatically handle device and advertising information as described above. If the app is distributed to children or includes children in its target audience, its advertising and age-related settings must comply with the requirements that apply to that audience and location. A parent or guardian may contact us with a concern using the contact details below.

International Users

The app's local document processing does not send your documents to Scanova. Google Mobile Ads may process advertising-related information as described in Google's policies and in accordance with its applicable terms and legal obligations. Privacy rights and requirements vary by location.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, SDKs, or data practices change. The effective and updated dates at the top of this document indicate the latest revision. If data practices change, the app's store disclosures should be updated as well.
